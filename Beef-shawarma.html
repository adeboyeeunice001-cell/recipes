BEEF SHAWARMA

Beef shawarma is a delicious wrap made with tender, well-seasoned strips of beef, fresh vegetables, and a creamy, flavorful sauce. The beef is usually marinated with spices, grilled or cooked until juicy, then wrapped in soft flatbread with ingredients such as cabbage, carrots, onions, tomatoes, and mayonnaise. It is a popular street food because it is filling, easy to eat, and packed with bold flavour.

INGREDIENTS

  • 500 g beef, thinly sliced
  • 4 shawarma bread or tortilla wraps
  • 1 cup cabbage, shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Vegetable oil for cooking

PROCEDURES

  1. Wash the beef and slice it into thin strips.
  2. Season the beef with garlic powder, paprika, curry powder, salt, and black pepper.
  3. Heat a little vegetable oil in a pan and cook the beef until it is browned and fully cooked.
  4. In a small bowl, mix the mayonnaise and ketchup to make the shawarma sauce.
  5. Spread the sauce over each shawarma bread or tortilla wrap.
  6. Add shredded cabbage, grated carrot, sliced onion, tomato, and cooked beef.
  7. Roll the wrap tightly, folding in the sides as you roll.
  8. Toast the shawarma in a dry pan for a few minutes until lightly golden.
  9. Serve warm and enjoy.
