SAUSAGE ROLLS

Sausage rolls are a popular savoury snack made by wrapping seasoned sausage meat in flaky pastry and baking until golden brown. They are enjoyed hot or cold and are commonly served at parties, picnics, and casual gatherings. Simple, filling, and versatile, sausage rolls can be made in many sizes and flavours.

INGREDIENTS

  • 500g sausage meat
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten (for glazing)
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped (optional)
  • Herbs or spices, such as thyme or paprika (optional)

PROCEDURES

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
  2. Roll out the puff pastry on a lightly floured surface.
  3. Season the sausage meat with salt, pepper, and any optional herbs or spices.
  4. Place the sausage meat in a long line along one edge of the pastry.
  5. Brush the pastry edges with beaten egg, then roll the pastry around the filling.
  6. Cut into equal-sized rolls and place them on a baking tray.
  7. Brush the tops with beaten egg.
  8. Bake for 20–25 minutes, until golden brown and cooked through.
  9. Allow to cool slightly before serving.
