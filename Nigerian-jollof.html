NIGERIAN JOLLOF

JOLLOF RICE AND BEEF

Nigerian jollof is a beloved West African rice dish cooked in a rich tomato-and-pepper sauce, often seasoned with onions, spices, stock, and a hint of smokiness. It is famous for its vibrant red-orange color, deep flavor, and the prized slightly charred “party jollof” taste from cooking over high heat. Usually served with chicken, beef, fish, plantain, or salad, it is a staple at celebrations and a source of proud debate across West Africa.

INGREDIENTS

For Nigerian Jollof With Beef, You'll Need:

  • 2 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 500 g beef, cut into pieces
  • 4-5 fresh tomatoes, blended
  • 2 red bell peppers, blended
  • 1-2 scotch bonnet peppers, to taste
  • 2 onions
  • 3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 3-4 cups beef stock or water
  • 1/2 cup of vegetable oil
  • 2-3 seasoning cubes
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder or 2 cloves fresh garlic
  • 1 teaspoon ginger, grated or powdered
  • 1 bay leaf (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

PROCEDURES

  1. Wash the beef, place it in a pot, and season with salt, seasoning cubes, onion, garlic, ginger, curry powder, and thyme.
  2. Add a little water and cook the beef until it becomes soft. Keep the beef stock aside for the rice.
  3. Heat vegetable oil in a large pot. Fry the cooked beef until brown, then set it aside.
  4. In the same pot, fry the chopped onions for a few minutes.
  5. Add the tomato paste and fry for about 2 minutes.
  6. Add the blended tomatoes, red bell peppers, and scotch bonnet peppers. Cook until the sauce becomes thick and the oil rises to the top.
  7. Add curry powder, thyme, bay leaf, seasoning cubes, salt, and black pepper. Stir well.
  8. Pour in the beef stock and allow it to boil.
  9. Wash the rice and add it to the boiling sauce. Stir gently and cover the pot.
  10. Cook on low heat until the rice is soft and has absorbed the sauce. Add a little water if needed.
  11. Add the fried beef, stir gently, and cook for another 5 minutes.
  12. Serve the Nigerian jollof rice with beef while warm.
